\begin{abstract}
We compare Lorentzian Einstein--Cartan gravity with constant complete Nieh--Yan coupling on fixed compact representatives of $T^3$, Nil, Sol and $S^3$. In the common-scale, two-amplitude family, torsion is auxiliary and $\chi$ organizes reduced trajectories and specified curvature scalars. The full connection equation and five-direction shape variation restrict real common-scale vacuum families to locally flat static ones. On the flat torus, the second-order obstruction to pure scale extends to a mixed scale--shear constraint cone; each direction is realized by an exact local vacuum family. For Nil and Sol, initial tracefree Ricci determines quadratic shape evolution and the quartic scale departure. Selected quadratic and cubic curvature contractions require $J_2,J_3$ in addition to $\chi$, recovering the spatial Ricci spectrum at a point. We derive degenerate branches of the inverse structure-matrix problem, global frames of the same flat torus and canonical endpoint correspondence, distinguishing reduced trajectories, full vacuum solutions, structure representations and boundary data.
\end{abstract}
