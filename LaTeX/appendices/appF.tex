\section{Revision note}

This version uses geometric torsion, complete NY normalization, the physical spherical radius and fixed compact quotients. The GHY sign, auxiliary momentum equations, internal-dual metric factors and volume normalizations of the first version are corrected. The five-parameter Jacobi condition and the class-A classification of Sol are made explicit. Conversions from the earlier implementation are given in the supplementary convention guide; the changelog records the version changes.

The earlier NY-dependent bulk mixing and conditional MX classification, universal dynamic Pontryagin zero, Lorentz-scalar interpretation of the unweighted internal sum, and Higgsing/response correspondence are withdrawn. The auxiliary solution, reduced trajectory classification and static $C=-9\chi$ relation are retained in the domains specified in Sections 4--6.

This version also develops time-dependent class-A curvature formulas, relative Pontryagin endpoint quantities, the full connection equation and the tracefree coframe residual on reduced trajectories. It adds homogeneous shape variation, exact realization of mixed torus tangents, short-time Nil and Sol coefficients, selected quadratic and cubic contractions with the Ricci inverse problem, global frames of the static torus and canonical endpoint correspondence. The main text and appendices specify the variation domains and boundary conditions of each conclusion.
