\section{Conclusions}

For Lorentzian EC gravity with constant complete NY coupling and no matter, cosmological or curvature-square term, we compared four fixed reference geometries. The common-scale torsion amplitudes are auxiliary and real reduced trajectories are organized by the sign of $\chi$. Combining the full connection and shape equations restricts real common-scale vacuum families to locally flat static ones.

With shape allowed, the second-order obstruction to pure scale on the flat torus becomes part of a mixed scale--shear cone. Each tangent on this cone is realized by an exact local vacuum family on the fixed lattice. For Nil and Sol, the initial tracefree Ricci tensor fixes quadratic shape evolution and $J_2$ fixes the quartic scale departure.

Restricted $\chi$ dependence holds for the specified common-scale, two-amplitude scalars and relative primitive. Selected quadratic contractions additionally require $J_2$, and cubic contractions require $J_3$. These three quantities recover the spatial Ricci spectrum at a point, but degenerate branches of the inverse structure-matrix problem are nonunique. The flat-torus frame comparison and canonical endpoint correspondence make the distinctions between structure representations, cohomology and boundary data explicit.

The arguments are contained in the main text and Appendices A--D; the supplement provides rederivation, saved-data rendering and numerical comparisons at specified points. General inhomogeneous fields, long-time anisotropic evolution, physical spectra or stability, classification of compact quotients, covariant-derivative invariants and responses to a dynamical NY coupling are outside the present claims.
