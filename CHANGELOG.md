# Revision history

## v2 — 2026-11-07

This version studies common-scale Lorentzian Einstein–Cartan reduction on four fixed homogeneous geometries, its boundary completion, dynamic curvature quantities and relation to the full field equations. It also develops the torsionless homogeneous shape extension and the curvature information carried by chi, J2 and J3. The changes from v1 are listed below.

### Corrections and withdrawn statements

- Replaced the complete-NY bulk mixing term and coupling-dependent mixed-branch classification with the exact boundary contribution at constant coupling. The auxiliary solution is uniquely torsionless for positive scale.
- Adopted geometric torsion, physical spherical radius and explicit compact quotient volumes. Distinguished a change of spherical scale from the earlier absolute-volume error.
- Corrected the signed spacelike GHY completion and gave the remaining endpoint variations and canonical momentum shifts.
- Replaced the extrapolation of static Pontryagin zeros by time-dependent curvature. Corrected the form-index metric weights in the internal-dual contraction; the old frame sum is not a Lorentz scalar.
- Imposed the Jacobi condition on the former five-parameter display and corrected Sol to unimodular class A, type VI₀.
- Replaced combined admissible/conditional labels by auxiliary solvability, real reduced trajectories and full-equation residuals. Withdrew the interpretation of Nil and Sol reduced trajectories as full vacuum solutions.
- Replaced the old Paper04 Higgsing/response correspondence by a comparison of structure matrices and closed one-forms.

### Retained and added results

- Retained the scalar-curvature classification of reduced trajectories and the absence of turning points within the specified source-free common-scale model. Identified the irregular canonical constraint presentation on the static torus shell.
- Retained the static relation $`C=-9\chi`$ for the covariant internal-dual scalar, with the stated geometric normalization.
- Derived the time-dependent curvature blocks for an arbitrary constant class-A structure matrix. Local NY, Pontryagin and internal-dual scalars depend on that matrix through chi in the specified two-amplitude family.
- Derived absolute CS and relative Pontryagin primitives. Distinguished their structure dependence, frame dependence and interval integrals.
- Evaluated the full connection and coframe equations. The torsionless Nil and Sol reduced trajectories retain a nonzero spatial tracefree Einstein residual.
- Compared equal-chi structure matrices with different tracefree Ricci and absolute CS reference values. Integrated quantities retain the independent quotient-volume factor.
- Derived the five-direction homogeneous shape variation after torsion elimination. Together with the full connection equation, it restricts real common-scale vacuum families to locally flat static ones.
- Extended the second-order obstruction to pure scale on the flat torus to a mixed scale–shear constraint cone, and realized each of its directions by an exact Kasner-type vacuum family on the fixed lattice.
- Derived the initial Nil and Sol vacuum evolution: the tracefree Ricci tensor fixes the quadratic shape evolution and J2 fixes the quartic departure from linear scale.
- Derived selected quadratic and cubic curvature contractions, which require J2 and J3 in addition to chi, and the reconstruction of the spatial Ricci spectrum at a point, including the degenerate branches of the inverse structure-matrix problem.
- Compared global frames of the same flat torus, distinguishing closed constant-coefficient forms from de Rham cohomology, and derived the canonical correspondence of endpoint representatives.
